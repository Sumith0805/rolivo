Operations Coordinator
Harbor Lane Logistics, Visakhapatnam

About the role
You will keep our day-to-day operations running smoothly by coordinating schedules, suppliers and team requests.

What you will do
- Coordinate daily schedules between drivers, warehouse staff and customers
- Follow up with suppliers on deliveries and paperwork
- Keep records accurate and up to date
- Answer customer questions by phone and email
- Support the operations manager with planning

Requirements
- Graduate in any discipline
- Excellent communication and organization
- Comfortable with basic office tools
- Ability to handle several tasks at once
