Embedded Firmware Engineer
Kestrel Devices, Chennai

About the role
We design battery-powered IoT sensors. You will write the firmware that runs on them and help bring each new board to life.

What you will do
- Write and debug embedded C for ARM Cortex-M microcontrollers
- Implement drivers for sensors over I2C, SPI and UART
- Develop firmware on an RTOS such as FreeRTOS
- Reduce power consumption and memory use
- Test boards with oscilloscopes and logic analyzers

Requirements
- Degree in electronics, electrical or computer engineering
- Strong C programming skills
- Experience with microcontrollers such as STM32 or ESP32
- Knowledge of communication protocols and hardware debugging
- Nice to have: Wi-Fi or Bluetooth connectivity experience
