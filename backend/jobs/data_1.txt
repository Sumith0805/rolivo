Data Analyst
Meridian Retail Group, Mumbai (hybrid)

About the role
Meridian's analytics team turns sales and customer data into decisions. You will own weekly reporting and answer business questions with data.

What you will do
- Write SQL queries to pull and clean data from our warehouse
- Build dashboards in Power BI for sales, inventory and marketing teams
- Prepare weekly and monthly reporting packs in Excel
- Run ad hoc analytics and present findings to non-technical stakeholders
- Maintain simple ETL jobs and check data quality

Requirements
- Degree in statistics, mathematics, engineering or a related field
- Strong SQL and Excel skills
- Experience with Power BI or Tableau and data visualization
- Clear written and spoken communication
- Nice to have: Python or R for analysis
