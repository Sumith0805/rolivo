SOC Analyst (Tier 1)
Sentinel Secure, Gurugram (shift based)

About the role
You will be part of our security operations center, watching over customer networks around the clock.

What you will do
- Monitor alerts in the SIEM and triage possible incidents
- Investigate suspicious activity and escalate real threats
- Review firewall and intrusion detection logs
- Run vulnerability scans and report findings
- Write clear incident reports and follow response playbooks

Requirements
- Degree in computer science, cybersecurity or related field
- Understanding of networking, TCP/IP and network security basics
- Familiarity with common attacks and defensive tools
- Certifications such as CCNA or Security+ are a plus
- Calm, careful and good at writing
